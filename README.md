### Why KDM

⚡ **Faster downloads.** Big files download in several parts at once.

⏯️ **Pause and resume.** Stop a download and pick it up later, even after a restart.

🌐 **Works with your browser.** Click a download link in Edge or Chrome and KDM takes over.

🎬 **Save videos too.** A **Download video** button appears on video players.

🗂️ **Stays tidy.** Files go into folders by type, and KDM warns you before you download the same file twice.

🕒 **On your schedule.** Set a speed limit, plan downloads for later, or let your PC shut down when everything is done.

🔗 **Grab many links at once.** Pick the files you want from a page, or paste a whole list.

### Get started in 1 minute

**1. Install.**
- **If Edge says the file "isn't commonly downloaded":** click the small arrow next to **Delete** (don't click Delete itself), then **Keep anyway**. Chrome: click **Keep**.
- Open the file. If Windows shows a blue box, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

These warnings only mean KDM is new, not that it's unsafe.

**2. Add KDM to your browser.** Open KDM and go to **How to use**. It walks you through it in four short steps.

**3. Download.** Download as usual. KDM does the rest.

Already have KDM? Install the new version over it. Your downloads and settings stay.
